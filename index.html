What We Do

One partner.
Three approaches.

Most distributors expanding into new markets face challenges when searching for the ideal location, including finding facilities of the right size and securing adequate outside storage. Cypress Point Partners closes that gap by covering the full lifecycle—from site identification to facility occupancy—ensuring clients benefit from consistent guidance and expertise with the same advisor supporting them at every stage. This continuity offers greater accountability, seamless communication, and a personalized approach that helps clients navigate each phase with confidence and efficiency.
01

Find It

Secure the right property for your needs today—and position your business for tomorrow's growth. Tap into our decades of industrial distribution experience to identify facilities that support your operations, fit your growth strategy, and help you move faster toward above-market revenue goals.

02

Buy It

Unlock the facility your growth strategy requires without tying up capital in brick and mortar. Cypress Point Partners acquires the right properties and delivers win-win lease structures that let growth companies stay focused on expansion, operations, and revenue generation. We understand what scaling companies need—and we move quickly to secure facilities that fit, including opportunities in markets where the best assets are for sale, not for lease.

03

Build It

We design and deliver a facility that works from day one. Specify the dock ratios, clear heights, yard space, eave heights, and column spacing your operation needs to run efficiently, scale confidently, and support long-term growth.

Our Company

One principal.
One focus.

Munroe Best has over 30 years’ experience in the construction products and building materials industry in family-owned, private equity backed and publicly traded companies. He is considered an expert in industrial distribution, turnarounds and acquisition integration.

Most recently, Best was the President of the South Division at Beacon Building Products, the $9.8 billion distributor of roofing and building materials with over 550 facilities across the U.S. and Canada. In that role, he led the $3.0B revenue and 2,100 employee South Division and served on Beacon’s Executive Committee, as well as its IT and Disclosure Committees.

Since joining Beacon following the acquisition of his family business, Best Distributing, in 2000, Munroe held progressively senior roles during a period of tremendous growth. During his tenure, Beacon moved from private equity ownership to a publicly traded company and grew revenue from $400 million to over $9.8 billion through acquisitions and organic growth initiatives.

He was an integral part of pre- and post-acquisition integration involving more than $600 million in businesses, led the turnaround of several struggling geographies, and helped roll out Beacon’s “Ambition 25” strategic plan, which contributed to significant shareholder value creation and the eventual $11 billion sale of the company to QXO in 2025.
